# Contributing

For a bug report, include your Windows version, app version, playback mode, steps to reproduce, and the text shown in Diagnostics. Remove any private information before sharing it. Conversation recordings are not needed.

For changes, keep audio capture and playback independent of the GUI, preserve Spotify's original volume and mute state, and run the checks in [BUILDING.md](docs/BUILDING.md). Explain the resulting behaviour and what you tested.
